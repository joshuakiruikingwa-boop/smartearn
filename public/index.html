Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the EarnPoint protocol.

What is EarnPoint?

EarnPoint is a premium freelance and rewards ecosystem designed to help users monetize their time through various tasks, surveys, and network growth opportunities.

How do I start earning?

Simply create an account, activate it by paying the nominal protocol fee, and you'll get immediate access to our task dashboard, including high-yield surveys and video rewards.

Why is there an activation fee?

The activation fee ensures a high-quality user base and helps sustain our 3-tier referral commission structure, allowing us to reward members who help grow the ecosystem.

How does the referral system work?

We utilize a 3-level commission protocol. You earn 10% from direct referrals (Level 1), 5% from their referrals (Level 2), and 2% from the next tier (Level 3).

How can I withdraw my earnings?

Withdrawals are processed directly to your registered M-Pesa account. Simply reach the minimum threshold and request a payout from your dashboard.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption and secure PostgreSQL database protocols to ensure your personal information and transaction history remain confidential.
